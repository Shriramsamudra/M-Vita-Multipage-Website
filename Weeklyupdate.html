Wealth Protection: Medical Insurance & Latest Rules

Before growing your money in stocks or mutual funds, you must protect your hard-earned savings from unexpected medical emergencies. Here is why health insurance is your primary family shield.

🏥

Why Medical Insurance is Non-Negotiable Today

Medical expenses in India grow at over 14% every year—twice as fast as regular inflation. A single hospital stay without adequate insurance can easily wipe out 3 to 5 years of mutual fund SIP gains.

Recent Regulatory News & Customer Benefits (IRDAI Updates):

👴 1. No Age Limit on Health Policies

IRDAI has removed the 65-year age limit on purchasing new health insurance policies. Senior citizens can now secure health insurance coverage regardless of age.

⏱️ 2. Shorter Pre-Existing Illness Waiting Period

The maximum waiting period for pre-existing illnesses (like diabetes or hypertension) has been reduced from 4 years to 3 years across all insurance companies.

⚡ 3. 1-Hour Cashless Claim Approvals

Hospitals and insurers are now mandated to process cashless pre-authorization within 1 hour, reducing emergency stress for patients and families.
