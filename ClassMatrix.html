Frequently Asked Questions

Can it schedule back-to-back double periods for labs?

Yes! You can easily set requirements for double periods (such as Science or Computer Labs), and the algorithm automatically ensures they are scheduled consecutively on the same day without breaking workloads.

How does it handle an absent teacher in the morning?

ClassMatrix includes a powerful "Proxy Finder." Simply select the absent teacher and the day. It instantly identifies their affected classes and shows you exactly who is free during those periods, prioritizing free teachers who teach the exact same subject.

Is there a limit on how many classes or teachers I can add?

Not at all. The software is built robustly to handle the complex workloads of both small primary schools and massive K-12 institutions with hundreds of faculty members and divisions.

Can I pin specific subjects to exact days and times?

Yes. The "Quota Matrix" module allows you to pin subjects like Morning Assembly, Mass PT, or Joint Sessions to an exact day and period. The algorithm then automatically schedules the rest of the week around your pinned rules.

Do I need an active internet connection to generate timetables?

No. ClassMatrix is a 100% offline desktop application. Your school's data remains entirely secure on your local hard drive, and the powerful generation algorithm runs locally on your PC without ever needing the cloud.
