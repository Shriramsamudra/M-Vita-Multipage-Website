Frequently Asked Questions

Is my client data safe?

Yes! The software is 100% offline. It runs a local database directly on your computer's hard drive. Your client's financial data never touches the internet or our servers.

What if I install it on a new computer?

The software license is hardware-bound using a unique Activation Code generated from your motherboard. If you buy a new computer, simply contact support to securely transfer your license key.

Does it handle monthly Trail Commissions properly?

Absolutely. The deduplication engine is smart enough to accept monthly trail commissions for the same transaction while strictly blocking identical files from inflating your numbers.
